# Piece rotation

Button clicks played when a piece is rotated (`rotateClick` in `src/sfx.js`). Opus in an Ogg container (this ffmpeg
build has no Vorbis encoder), 48 kHz, 96 kbps, with a 1 ms fade in and 6 ms fade out so no clip starts or stops on a
non-zero sample. Originals are in `art/audio-src/ui/`.
