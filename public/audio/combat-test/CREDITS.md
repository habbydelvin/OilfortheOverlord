# Combat Test sound effects

Built by `tools/build-combat-audio.py` from originals kept in `art/audio-src/` (not shipped): cut, short edge fades,
resampled to 48 kHz, one static gain to a loudness target, at least 1 dB below full scale. No dynamic processing.
