Combat test

Zoom out past max (or press War Map) to see the planet. Click a rival city to enter it, drag a missile card from your hand onto one of its tiles to aim it (click an aimed tile to take it back). Your hand holds one missile per armed silo slot, refilled every turn, then End turn: every city fires, the flights cross the planet, then strikes on Hive City play here. Next turn sends missiles in, filmed with the game's strike camera. Each Intercept Network is a CIWS bunker: its turret swings onto the missile and fills the sky with rounds. A hit brings it down in mid-air; a miss streams past behind it. Drag / scroll / WASD to move the camera.
